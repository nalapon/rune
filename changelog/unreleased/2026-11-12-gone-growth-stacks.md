- **Growth: a stack whose exit finished keeps nothing, and nothing out of sight reads as moving.** A `GrowthSpec` stack that exited left its own state and its rows' heights behind, so one that came back at the same path grew in the rows it lacked and closed gaps for rows it no longer had, where a new stack mounts settled. It now comes back as a new stack does. A stack or row that left while moving, or a virtual row scrolled out of sight mid-growth, also read as moving for the rest of the run, so hover stopped holding still while layout moved; only what is mounted counts now. What leaves at once (`Tx::instant`) still keeps its state, to come back as it was.
