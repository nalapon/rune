- **Text fields: a right-to-left by-paragraph field keeps its direction beside a label of the same text.** A one-line `El::dir_by_paragraph` field turned its shape key's direction a second time, which cancelled it, so it shared a cached layout with an `Auto` text of the same words and style: a line with no strong character, such as `123`, took whichever direction was shaped first. The field's paragraphs also record the text's own shape key now, so an edit of a right-to-left field drops the layouts it replaced instead of keeping them until the cache's byte budget evicts them.
