- **Text fields: typing in a long multi-line field shapes only the line it changes.** A wrapped multi-line field now lays its text out a paragraph at a time, as `El::dir_by_paragraph` fields already did, whenever its lines draw as its one layout would, and an edit keeps the layouts of the lines it left alone: a keystroke in a 50 KB note went from 19 ms to 0.3 ms of CPU (headless, `--profile perf`). Its lines read in the field's one direction: its own, or for `Auto` its whole text's first strong one. A field keeps one layout when it has links, highlights or rings, isn't start-aligned, has list, quote or code blocks, a span that sizes text or a strike over a newline, or holds a CR, a bidi control, or kana, Hangul, Bopomofo or Han. Each line's top is rounded to a device pixel, so a line can sit up to half a pixel from where the one layout put it. A by-paragraph field's line also finds the spans and pills over it by halving, not by reading them all.
