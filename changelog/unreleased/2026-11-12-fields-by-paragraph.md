- **Text fields: a multi-line field lays out by paragraph only when its lines draw as its one layout would.** A field that isn't `El::dir_by_paragraph` keeps one layout when it has links, highlights or rings, isn't start-aligned, has list, quote or code blocks, a span that sizes text or a strike over a newline, or holds a CR, a bidi control, or kana, Hangul, Bopomofo or Han: one layout places, sizes or shapes those from the whole text. Its lines read in the field's one direction, for `Auto` the whole text's first strong character's rather than its first line's, so `123⏎مرحبا` reads right to left as one layout does; its mention pills aren't isolated, and it shares no cached layout with a `dir_by_paragraph` field of the same text. A line finds the spans and pills over it by halving, not by reading them all.
